Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

"Clip Poster" (the "App") is a personal desktop tool that uploads short gaming highlight videos to its owner's TikTok account. This policy explains what data it handles.

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic): your display name and avatar, shown in the App so you can confirm which account you are posting to.
  • Access tokens: issued by TikTok when you log in, used only to upload videos you have chosen to post (video.publish / video.upload).

How it is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer. The App has no server, no database in the cloud, and no analytics. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Videos

Videos are created and stored on the owner's computer and are sent only to TikTok, when you post them.

Deleting your data

Revoke the App's access in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the App's folder from the computer. Because nothing is stored elsewhere, that removes everything.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.
